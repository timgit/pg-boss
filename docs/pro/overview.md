# Overview

Pro's overview is the free dashboard's [overview](../dashboard.md#overview) with its own additions. When any alerts need somebody, a line at the top says how many and links to [Alerts](./alerts.md). Below it are the free overview's figures and queues, then the instances running, the last thirty days of status, and recent activity (operations and changes from the [audit log](./audit-log.md)).
